About

Deck design, from outline to final export.

I work with founders, marketing teams, and speakers to turn a messy outline into a deck that actually gets read — clear structure, one idea per slide, and a visual system that holds together for 60 slides or six. Every project on this page went out the door and got presented for real.

My process usually starts with your existing content: a doc, a script, or last year's deck. I restructure the narrative first, then design the system it lives in — so the deck reads well before it ever looks good.
